## Summary
The codebase follows a reasonably clean layered structure (components/hooks/lib/pages) with good reuse of shared UI primitives (ScreenScaffold, Card, StateView, Amount). However, it shows clear signs of being assembled by multiple independent generation passes: a stale/misleading lib/contract.ts that conflicts with real implementations, duplicated haptic-feedback and number-formatting boilerplate spread across many files, and one page (CompanyForm) that bypasses the shared useAppState state-management pattern used everywhere else. These are addressable with light refactoring rather than a redesign.
